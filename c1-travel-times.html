"No One Is
Coming For Us"
600,000 tourists stranded across 16 countries as Thomas Cook collapses overnight — phones go unanswered
Empty Thomas Cook check-in desks at Manchester Airport, September 23, 2019.

"I'm not going to pay for my holiday again. No one from Thomas Cook has appeared since the rumours started."

— J. Robsson, stranded in Mallorca

At 2am on September 23, 2019, Thomas Cook — a 178-year-old company that invented the package holiday — quietly ceased to exist. By morning, 600,000 tourists across Spain, Greece, Turkey, Tunisia and beyond had no flights, no answers, and no one picking up the phone.

Some were locked inside hotels in Tunisia, with staff refusing to let guests leave until unpaid bills were settled. Others arrived at airport desks to find them empty, branded signage already stripped away.

The British government launched its largest peacetime repatriation in history — flying in aircraft from as far as Malaysia. But for many, the damage was already done: savings gone, hotels demanding double payment, and a company that had been selling holidays until the very last day.

Language in Focus
Negative & Limiting Adverbials · Inversion for Emphasis
When a sentence begins with a negative or limiting adverbial (Never, Little, Not until, Not only, Only when, No way), the auxiliary verb inverts with the subject.
e.g. Never had I imagined this could happen.  ·  Only when the results arrived did I realise...
